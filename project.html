Easy Recipes

Simple food you can make at home

Tomato Pasta

Cooking time: 20 minutes

Ingredients

  • 200g pasta
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 1 garlic clove
  • Salt and olive oil

Preparation

  1. Boil the pasta.
  2. Cook garlic and tomatoes in oil.
  3. Mix with pasta and serve.

Fruit Yogurt Bowl

Cooking time: 5 minutes

Ingredients

  • 1 cup yogurt
  • 1 banana
  • Berries
  • Honey

Preparation

  1. Put yogurt in a bowl.
  2. Add banana and berries.
  3. Add honey on top.

Cheese Sandwich

Cooking time: 10 minutes

Ingredients

  • 2 bread slices
  • 2 cheese slices
  • Butter

Preparation

  1. Butter the bread.
  2. Place cheese inside.
  3. Toast until golden.

Vegetable Salad

Cooking time: 10 minutes

Ingredients

  • 1 cucumber
  • 1 tomato
  • 1 carrot
  • Salt and lemon juice

Preparation

  1. Chop the vegetables.
  2. Put them in a bowl.
  3. Add salt and lemon juice.

Chocolate Milkshake

Cooking time: 5 minutes

Ingredients

  • 1 glass milk
  • 2 scoops ice cream
  • 1 spoon cocoa powder

Preparation

  1. Put everything in a blender.
  2. Blend for one minute.
  3. Pour into a glass.
